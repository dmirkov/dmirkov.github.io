Dragan Mirkov

Full Professor, Faculty of Sport and Physical Education, University of Belgrade

Research

  • Assessment of neuromuscular function — strength, power and rate of force development
  • Force–velocity profiling in sprinting, jumping and isokinetic dynamometry
  • Validation of new measurement technologies against reference methods
  • Sprint biomechanics
  • Monitoring of rehabilitation after anterior cruciate ligament reconstruction (ACLR)

Positions

  • Full Professor, Faculty of Sport and Physical Education, University of Belgrade
  • Visiting Professor, Faculty of Dental Medicine and Health Osijek, Josip Juraj Strossmayer University of Osijek
  • Co-founder, Kinetics Hub (nonprofit organization for sports diagnostics)
  • Scientific Advisor, Onescrin
  • Scientific Advisor, Clavius Health

Education

  • BSc in Physics
  • MSc in Biophysics
  • PhD in Sports Sciences
