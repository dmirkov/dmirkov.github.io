Statistics in Physical Education and Sport

Statistical methods for research in physical education and sport: from describing data and the fundamentals of statistical inference to correlation and regression, the t test, analysis of variance, measurement reliability and nonparametric tests. The course follows the textbook by Weir and Vincent (2021).
